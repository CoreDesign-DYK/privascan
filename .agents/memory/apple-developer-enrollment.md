---
name: Apple Developer enrollment status
description: How to interpret the external Apple enrollment confirmations before continuing with Xcode and TestFlight.
---

An Apple Store order confirmation followed by an App Store Connect confirmation indicates that the Developer Program enrollment flow has provisioned access; the next work can move to Xcode team setup and App Store Connect app registration.

**Why:** The purchase receipt confirms the order, while the App Store Connect message confirms that the developer account is being recognized for app distribution services.

**How to apply:** Verify the same Apple Account in Xcode and App Store Connect before creating the app record or archiving the iOS project.
